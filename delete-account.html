Delete Your Account & Data

App: SpeakIt (Android and iPhone) · Developer: Benzi Heler · Last updated: October 1, 2026

Delete it yourself, in the app

  1. Open SpeakIt and go to Settings.
  2. Make sure you are signed in, then tap Delete account and confirm.

If the app asks you to sign in again first, that is a security check by our sign-in provider — sign in and tap Delete account again.

Or ask us by email

If you cannot open the app, or you want to remove a leaderboard entry, email us. We will act within 30 days.

benziheler@gmail.com

Please include the email address you used to sign in (or, for a leaderboard entry, the name, score and date shown on it).

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in account (email, name, profile picture and sign-in provider).
  • Your Friends data, if you used Friends: your game progress shared with friends, your approximate area, your friend codes and your friend links.

What is not deleted

  • Game progress on your phone stays on that phone. To remove it, reset progress in Settings or uninstall the app.
  • Leaderboard entries are not linked to your account, so they are not removed automatically. Email us and we will remove them.
  • Problem reports you sent contain no account details and are kept as long as needed to fix the problem.
  • Your Facebook authorization stays on Facebook’s side; remove SpeakIt in Facebook’s “Apps and websites” settings.
  • Duel rooms are deleted automatically, normally within 2 days of being created.
  • Analytics and crash data held by Google expire after limited periods set by Google (crash reports about 90 days; analytics event data at most 14 months).
